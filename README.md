# CivicVoice AI
### Your Voice. Your City. Your Solution.

**CivicVoice AI** is a voice-powered smart civic issue reporting and management platform that makes it easier for citizens to report problems such as potholes, damaged roads, garbage overflow, drainage issues, and water leakage.

### Problem
Traditional civic complaint systems can be difficult to access and use. Language barriers and complicated reporting processes can discourage citizens from reporting local issues.

### Our Solution
CivicVoice AI allows citizens to report civic issues through voice in multiple languages, including English, Hindi, and Kannada.

### How It Works
1. **Voice Input:** Citizens record their complaint in their preferred language.
2. **Speech-to-Text:** Gnani AI Prisma converts the spoken complaint into text.
3. **Smart Classification:** A rule-based engine identifies the complaint category, severity, and priority.
4. **Complaint Registration:** Citizens provide their location and email address, and the system generates a unique complaint ID.
5. **Voice Response:** Gnani AI Timbre generates spoken confirmation in the selected language.
6. **Email Notifications:** Citizens receive complaint confirmations and status updates, including voice attachments.
7. **Complaint Tracking:** Citizens can track their complaint status using their complaint ID.
8. **Administrator Dashboard:** Administrators can review complaints and update their status to Submitted, In Progress, or Resolved.

### Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TypeScript
- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
- **Voice AI:** Gnani AI Prisma (Speech-to-Text) and Timbre (Text-to-Speech)
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Additional Tools:** Leaflet Maps, email notifications, Vercel and Render

### Impact
CivicVoice AI aims to make civic reporting more accessible, inclusive, and convenient by combining multilingual voice AI with complaint management and tracking.
